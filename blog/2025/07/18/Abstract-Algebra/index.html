July 18, 2025

This is a study record of a course offered by Harvard University as an online course. The course information is as follows:

  • Instructor: Prof. Benedict Gross
  • Videos: YouTube
  • Textbook: Algebra by Michael Artin

I find that grasping new and complex concepts isn’t particularly difficult, but fully understanding the entire theory and the connections between key ideas is much more challenging. I’ve spent too much time resting… Now, I want to push myself to study something in depth — not necessarily something I need to finish quickly, but something I can take time to truly understand.
